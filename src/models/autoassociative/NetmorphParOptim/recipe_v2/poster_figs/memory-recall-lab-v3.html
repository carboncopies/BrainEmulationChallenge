Memory Recall Lab

This is an engram-based autoassociative memory: four 8-cell patterns are stored in a simulated network, and a partial cue (4 of a pattern's 8 cells) makes the other 4 fire. The patterns are written directly into synaptic weights; nothing is trained. The network is a NetMorph-grown CA3-like network of 96 pyramidal cells and 48 interneurons, simulated in BrainGenix-NES. Its growth parameters stay inside the hard biological limits, with the synapse-distance cap D_synmax set to the team guidance (1.5 um for pyramidal pairs, 0.1 um for interneuron pairs). Everything below is real simulation output from one network realisation (seed 20240628) and one run per condition.

GABA silenced

With every inhibitory (GABA) connection set to zero, single-memory recall is identical to the intact network (all spike times and counts the same). In the 12 ms ripple the outcome and every spike count are the same, and timing differs by under 0.1 ms (last spike 40.06 ms instead of 40.15 ms). In this network the interneurons fire a little (1 to 3 spikes per recall) but have no effect on the result.

What this shows and what it does not

What it shows

  • It works as an excitation-only memory. The four cue cells deliver 16.5 nS to each other member, which crosses the firing threshold; three cells deliver 12.4 nS, which does not.
  • Bursts end by themselves. Each cell's own adaptation (a rising threshold after each spike) stops the loop within about 4 ms at the 1 ms step (7.5 ms at 0.1 ms), so four memories can be cued one after another without any one of them running on.
  • It stays selective under a small background, up to a cap of 0.15 nS per connection.

What it does not show

  • Inhibition does not shape the ripple. Only 2.2% of synapses in this network are inhibitory, only 7 of 48 interneurons can be driven to firing by an 8-cell volley, and 1 to 3 interneurons fire per recall. Silencing GABA changes nothing.
  • The GABA weight cannot be set through the NES interface (it can only be zeroed), so stronger inhibition could not be tested.
  • Realistic inhibitory dynamics would need a settable GABA weight in NES and about 50 to 60 inhibitory synapses per pyramidal cell, which this network does not have.

Open questions and caveats

  • Weights below 0.1 are not a limit breach: Randal Koene confirmed on 2026-11-13 that the 0.1 to 1.0 dm.weight range applies to the growth parameter only, not to individual synapse weights (4178 of 5533 pyramidal-to-pyramidal pairs at background 0.15, and 5389 exact zeros in the pruned variant, are fine).
  • Spines are not yet drawn in the EM output.
  • The D_synmax maxima (1.5 um pyramidal pairs, 0.15 um interneuron pairs, 0.1 used) were confirmed by Randal Koene on 2026-11-13.
  • One network realisation, one run per condition, memories placed by a search over this network's connectome (not discovered by training).

Phase I: PV-basket arbor test and scaled inhibition (14 Nov)

Arbor test (seed 20240628, 96 pyramidal, D_synmax 1.5 / 0.1, hard limits respected)

  • Interneuron lengths can be matched to the PV literature with B_inf alone: dendrite about 7,100 um per cell (literature 5,000-9,000); axon B_inf 20 gives about 45,000 um (literature 30,000-45,000), against about 77,500 um at B_inf 26.
  • That does not fix the circuit. Synapses per Int-to-Pyr pair stay at 1.4-1.7 (PV literature about 11), each interneuron reaches 11-19 of 96 pyramidal cells, and the axon stays within about 125-133 um of the soma in every variant (node distance; the earlier 49 um figure counted growth cones only).
  • Best variant drives 10 of 48 interneurons with an 8-cell volley (bar: 16). Variants closer on a looser count change Pyr-Pyr by more than 5 percent and are rejected. One seed per variant, about +/-20 percent noise; the explanation (D_synmax 0.1 cap plus small disc) is inference.

Scaled inhibition (reduced-model scaling, non-natural per-connection strength)

  • Pyr-to-Int AMPA x3-x5 with GABA x5 (4.6-7.7x and 2.0x the literature per-pair values) gives inhibition a measurable role; at natural strength it has none.
  • Without it the network runs away at about 0.6 nS background per pair; with it the network stays clean to about 0.8-0.9 nS (0.65-0.7 on a second seed). That background is itself above our 0.15 nS cap.
  • At 37.5 percent engram overlap, silenced or natural GABA lets a cue fire 2-3 non-shared cells of the other memory; scaled inhibition removes it on one network and reduces it on the other. Own-engram completion stays complete.
  • Recall holds up to x5; x7 starts to cost recall and x10 breaks it. Burst-end effect is weak. Per-cell totals stay below derived literature totals (about 0.07-0.11x interneuron input, 0.45x pyramid inhibition). The mechanism (Pyr-Int-Pyr feedback) is inference.

Inspiration and reference

  • Weight-space programming: Rikiya Takehi*, Jayden Teoh*, Alex Zhang and Omar Khattab (MIT CSAIL), "Sparks of Weight-Space Programming: LLMs can natively read & write neural network weights", blog post, 2 November 2026, https://rikiyat.github.io/blog/weight-space-programming/. A blog post, not a peer-reviewed paper. It describes the concept as treating a network's weights like a program that is read and written, and says it is a concept, not a method. We use the idea (set weights by reasoning about the model, with no gradient descent); we do not reproduce its numbers, and our networks are spiking NES neurons.
  • The post itself cites earlier related work (for example Karpathy's "Software 2.0", fast weight programmers, and weight compilers), so it is not the origin of the underlying idea. The full list of related work and the NetMorph sources is in section 2 of the write-up on branch netmorph-weightspace-fable.

Phase G and team replies

Phase G: robustness and inhibition

  • Robustness: the recipe was used unchanged on two new seeds (20240629 and 20240630, K = 4) and on K = 5 and 6 (seed 628). Every single recall and ripple (12, 16, 20 ms and shuffled) passes. A 3-cell cue never completes in any single run. Cross-talk and spurious pyramidal spikes are 0 in every run, and GABA-silenced runs are spike-for-spike identical.
  • GABA editing works through a patched copy of the model file: ModelLoad honours GABA weight x2, x2.5 and x5 and faster kinetics (0.3 / 3.3 ms). Recall and ripple stay identical or within 0.6 ms in burst end, so inhibition has no role at any tested strength. Raising Pyr to interneuron drive up to 1.95x (non-natural) also changes nothing: interneurons fire after the 8-spike pyramidal burst has ended.
  • Hypothesis, not tested: at the 128-pyramidal limit each interneuron receives too few inputs (about 11.9 pyramidal pairs, 15.8 synapses; 13.7 GABA synapses per pyramidal cell). This is an inference.
  • All of these are deterministic single runs. Not tested: Dt 0.1 on the new seeds, K above 6, background above 0.15.

Interneuron literature

  • PV basket cells are the most likely source of lateral inhibition: fast, large, short-latency IPSCs onto pyramidal cells and strong reciprocity between PV cells. Axo-axonic cells rank second; CCK basket, OLM and ivy cells are slow or dendritic.
  • PV basket cell dendrite is about 5,000 to 9,000 um and axon about 30,000 to 45,000 um (working range from slice reconstructions, lower bounds). Our grown axon cones stay within about 49 um (node reach is about 130 um, see Phase I).
  • NeuroMorpho's length field is axon plus dendrite when an axon is traced, so it overstates dendritic length. Several figures are recalled or from summarised pages and need a spot-check.

Replies from Randal Koene, 2026-11-13

  • The proposed hard D_synmax limits make sense (maximum only, minimum 0): pyramidal pairs 1.5 um, interneuron pairs 0.15 um, 0.1 used.
  • He knows of no dataset source for CA3 interneuron morphometrics. Check the definition of total dendritic length; the HBP or BlueBrain reconstructions may help.
  • No preferred reference. Check the CA3 interneuron types and which one does lateral inhibition.
  • The gap from natural is interneuron arbor extent and the high proportion of pyramidal cells contacted. NetMorph's default interneuron outgrowth may be wrong for PV basket cells, because its statistics come from cultures.
  • dm.weight applies only to the growth parameter, so 0.1 to 1.0 is not a per-synapse weight limit. The 0.15 nS background weights are not a breach.
  • He would consider exposing GABA and NMDA settings: GABA with a maximum conductance and a weight of 0 to 1. NMDA matters mostly for learning.

Sensitivity study

An earlier network with D_synmax of 0.5 um for interneuron pairs (67 pyramidal cells, 27 interneurons) is not biologically realistic and is kept only as a mechanism study. There, inhibition did matter: the ripple passed with strong cross-inhibition between memories. On the realistic network above, the design calculation puts cross-inhibition 5 to 20 times below what one memory would need to veto another, so that mechanism is not available.
