(B) Weight-0 test. Grow E4 with pyramidal D_synmax 0.035 (B1) and 0.05 (B2), interneuron D 0.03, both seeds (arbors identical to baseline by the linefix result; synapse counts rise). The dump carries synapse counts per pair (num_receptors) but not per-synapse distances, so "set weak synapses to weight 0" is realised at synapse level by random thinning (rule R): each Pyr->Pyr synapse of a pair is kept with probability r = (baseline Pyr->Pyr synapse total of the same seed)/(total of the higher-D model), n_kept ~ Binomial(n, r) with fixed RNG seed 20261122; the pair's effective conductance is g * n_kept/n (pair weight n_kept/n, zero when n_kept = 0, since NES stores one weight per pair and conductance is linear in synapse count). Rule W (secondary, offline bars only): zero the pairs with the fewest synapses first until the total matches. Bars are computed on the effective connectome (a thinned copy of the viz dump run through the unchanged analysis code); recall runs (dyn2 logic, D42 designs from feas2 on the effective connectome, c = 0.15 and 1.5, GABA on) use rule R weights on the real sim, plus the unthinned higher-D models at the same c as comparator. Pyr->Int and interneuron pairs are not thinned. Limitation stated in advance: real D reduction removes the synapses with the largest crossing distance, which the dump does not record, so random thinning is an approximation of "same effective count", not of lowering D.

## Scoring and pass/fail rules (fixed now)
- Predictions scored HIT inside the stated range/direction, PARTIAL within 25% beyond, MISS otherwise.
- (A) lever "works" for a bar/outcome only if the effect exceeds the existing E4 seed noise (above) in both seeds with the same sign; otherwise "no demonstrated effect". Success of A as an E4 improvement: any arm that gives all 7 bars on both seeds AND drivable >= baseline on both. Failures (growth crash, syntax not accepted, arbor identical to baseline = lever ignored) are reported as results.
- (B) thinning is "equivalent" to lowering D only if the thinned bars all pass wherever the D 0.0175 baseline passes and recall is within one memory-trial of baseline on matched seeds; otherwise not equivalent.
- (C) "unrealistic" flags are quantitative thresholds above plus human inspection of the PNGs, described in words; PNGs are evidence, not a measurement of realism (no real tissue reference).
