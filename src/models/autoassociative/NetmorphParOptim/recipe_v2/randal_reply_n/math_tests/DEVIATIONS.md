# math_tests deviations (numbered; none changes a preregistered decision rule after seeing results)
- D1 (C1 volume): no exact or telescoping volume estimate. Reported Chebyshev radius (LP) and hit-and-run (20,000 steps, no rounding) per-pair ranges relative to the box on the 10 lowest-margin cells of s66666/s77777 with P = 4 random memories (no input cap). Cells with margin 0 have zero radius and are reported as pinned.
- D2 (C3): the log-weight convex program (iv of the preregistration) was replaced by (a) the analytic bound rho >= 16 T_eff / m for m-of-17 completion and (b) a random local search for the cascade-minimal rho, run only on the A17 assemblies of s66666 and s77777 (not all 10 graphs), at T_eff 13.6 and 15.0 and 98% completion of 400 random cues. The AUC uses 22 (model, design) outcomes transcribed by hand from neuron_reset_ahp README steps B2-D (stability's 14-arm table has no termination column for the E/L designs); the script prints the AUC with the opposite sign convention (0.08 = 0.92).
- D3 (C1): the 3T cap runs made every draw infeasible through hub cells (lower weight bound alone above the cap); I report the cell-level feasible fraction next to the joint one and did not exclude hubs from the constraints.
- D4 (C6): the L1-sparse variant was not run (time box); the max-min slack LP was run as the per-member cutting-plane LP of C7 and used for design LPS.
- D5 (C7): hit-and-run volume ratio of the 75% vs the 50% polytope not computed (the 50% polytope is empty for most members).
- D6 (C2/C8): the machine load average was 250-570 (fable-knobs growths plus others) so the exact solves were starved. The first launches (8 graphs in parallel, 30 greedy restarts, 500 s MILP limit) did not finish in 35 min and were killed; relaunched with 4 greedy restarts of 8 and limits (LP TL/3, MILP TL) on the graphs listed in PHASE1_RESULTS. Graphs not solved within the limit are reported as unsolved, not as equal to greedy. ovlib.pack greedy (k = 0) undercounts compared with the feas2 packer used in the repo (baseline 2-3 vs 2-4).
- D7 (C4): only as-grown unprogrammed weights (0.1 G and 0.2 G, T = 15) were used for the percolation comparison; the programmed-design cascade (lp_weights validate_r3, 94-96% agreement with the sims) already exists and was not repeated.
- D8 (C5): K42 values for the 27 graphs come from a quick greedy (4-5 restarts); they are lower bounds with granularity 1; exact K42 exists only for the graphs where C2 finished.
- D9 (shell): three of my own bash commands killed themselves (pkill/pgrep -f patterns matching the command line, exit 144); no other worker's processes were touched: kills were by PID from `ps ... | grep "[v]env/bin/python c2"` and only of my own c2_exact processes.
- D10 (C11): exploratory reduced-model screen added after the preregistration (E20 x neuron levers); labelled exploratory, not scored against a prediction, not simulated.
- D11 (phase 2 timing): the first attempt to start the queue (before fable-knobs' batch ended) was refused by the permission system; I did not work around it. The queue was gated on "no gen_autonm process" and opened at 08:36 on 2026-11-25. No change to the preregistered design. PASS_T for phase 2 uses 50% >= 3/6 (my preregistration), weaker than analyze_nr's 4/6 PASS_T; no design passes either.
- D12: scoring was run with analyze_d.summ (PASS75 as defined there); the "PASS_T50" column is added by p2_score.py.
