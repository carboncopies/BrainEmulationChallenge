## C1. Per-cell perceptron LP with max-margin, feasibility vs number of patterns, hit-and-run volume
Computed: for each graph, memories drawn from `ovlib.candidates(P, 2)` (4-cue sets with >= 2 strong common targets), random subsets of P = 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32 memories (20 draws each, overlap unrestricted); per target cell the LP: variables = in-weights on existing pairs; constraints: completion sum_{cue} w >= T + s for each memory where the cell is non-cue; silence: every 3-subset of the cue <= theta + ... i.e. <= (T - 4.125) - ... exactly lp_weights primary (theta = 12.375) minus s for cells that are non-cue members; whole-memory input <= theta - s for cells outside the memory; box and input cap as above; maximize s. s* >= 0 means feasible, s* < 0 infeasible. Report fraction of draws jointly feasible and fraction of cells with s* >= 0 vs P, with and without the cap, P_c (largest P with >= 50% of draws feasible), the cells with minimal s*, and the Spearman of s* with in-degree and with strong in-degree. Hit-and-run volume (50 000 steps, Chebyshev-rounded) on the 10 lowest-margin cells of s66666 and s77777: log volume ratio to the box and per-pair ranges (pinned pairs).
Decision rule: (a) if the binding cells (lowest s*) are the lowest strong-in-degree cells in >= 8 of 10 graphs (Spearman of s* with strong in-degree >= 0.5), the growth-side target is "no starved cells"; (b) if P_c(no cap) is within a factor 1.5 of the packer counts of lp_weights Hinf (k <= 3) in >= 8 of 10 graphs the packer was not the bottleneck.
Prediction (P-C1): (a) holds in 6 of 10 graphs (45%); (b) 7 of 10 (40%); cap-3T P_c <= 3 in 9 of 10 graphs (80%); volume is thin (Chebyshev radius < 0.2 nS on the 10 worst cells) for >= 50% of those cells (60%).

## C2. Exact ILP / clique / packing vs the greedy count
Computed: for each graph, K42 (max disjoint 4 cue + 2 non-cue memories) with an exact solver. Enumerate candidates `ovlib.candidates(P, 2)` (cue C with its target list; a memory = (C, any 2 of its targets)); conflict = share a cell. Solve max set packing by HiGHS (scipy.optimize.milp) with a 10 min limit (node/time cap, 4 threads), on the candidate set reduced by dominance (for a cue set keep the memory choices only per target pair, but since only the cue set matters for conflicts after fixing targets, use variables x_(C, n1, n2)). Compare with the stored greedy lower bounds (`headline_i/feas_V1n20_s*.json` max_disjoint_m2 and the other 5 seeds' feas files) and report solve time, optimality gap, and for the cases where greedy < exact the instance. Also K44 if time.
Decision rule (file): if exact packing equals greedy on >= 8 of 10 graphs, the limit is the graph structure; otherwise the greedy packer under-counts and the stored capacities are lower bounds only.
Prediction (P-C2): exact equals greedy on 7 of 10 (40%); exact >= greedy on 10/10 (certain by definition); an exact solve finishes under 10 min on >= 8 of 10 (60%); the largest gain is <= 1 memory on every graph (70%).

## C3. Spectral design (rho window, contraction margin)
Computed: on the A17 member matrices of s66666/s77777 (all stored arms: L0, LH, W70, W50, H2) and the E4 strong-pair candidate memories' union: (i) rho of the member AMPA matrix and Chung-Lu-Vu estimate d~ = sum w^2/sum w, ratio rho/d~; (ii) the scaling rule rho(cW) = c rho(W) to hit rho in {16.0, 16.5, 17.1}; (iii) the post-adaptation contraction margin g_eff * rho with the adapted threshold (+1 mV per spike floor, 500 ms), compared with the stored termination times (14 arms with known lifetimes from `stability/score_table.md`); (iv) the log-weight convex program: minimise log rho(exp U) subject to the linear completion constraints (fixed Perron vector inner bound, per-pair boxes) on 75%-cue completion for the 17-cell assemblies, report the minimum rho for a threshold-model 75%-completing design per graph and compare with the ignition level 17.4 and completion level 15.8.
Decision rule: if the minimum rho for a threshold-model 75%-completing design is > 17.4 nS for >= 5 of 10 graphs, the threshold-model design cannot complete and stay silent there; if rho vs stored termination (lifetime < 100 ms vs not) separates arms with AUC >= 0.9 the rule is retained for phase 2.
Prediction (P-C3): min rho for completion < 17.4 on >= 7 of 10 graphs (65%); AUC of rho for termination >= 0.9 on the 14 stored arms (70%); rho/d~ between 0.5 and 1.5 (50%).
