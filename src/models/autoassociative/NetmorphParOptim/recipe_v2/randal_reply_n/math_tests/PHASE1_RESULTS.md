## Per candidate: what was computed, what it delivered, prediction scoring
### C1 per-cell perceptron LP (random draws of strong-pair memories, P = 1..32; 12 draws each; margin LP per cell; Chebyshev radius and hit-and-run on the 10 lowest-margin cells)
- With the homeostatic input cap (3T = 49.5 nS) the random draws are jointly infeasible for every P >= 1 on 9 of 10 graphs (P_c = 0; s55555: 1) because the graph's hub cells already exceed the cap at the lower weight bound (same finding as lp_weights). 96.6-97.8% of cells are individually feasible at P = 8, so it is the few hub cells, not the memories, that block.
- Without the cap, the largest P with >= 50% of random draws jointly feasible is P_c = 16, 16, 8, 16, 4, 16, 16, 8, 8, 8 (s11111, s16180, s22222, s27182, s31415, s33333, s44444, s55555, s66666, s77777). Random memory sets saturate at 4-16 memories, against 71 (median, overlap <= 3) for optimised selection in lp_weights (Hinf): selecting memories matters about 5-10x; the LP-feasibility test itself does not.
- The binding cells are not the starved ones. Spearman of the per-cell margin with strong in-degree is -0.48 to -0.73 in all 10 graphs (median strong in-degree is 0, since most cells have no strong input): the lowest-margin cells are the high-strong-in-degree cells that sit in many constraints. Decision rule (a) (binding = lowest strong in-degree, Spearman >= 0.5 in >= 8/10) FAILS (0/10); the opposite sign holds.
- Volume: on s66666 and s77777 the 4 lowest-margin cells of each (cells 14, 28, 30, 65 and 3, 70, 67, 65: 28, 65, 3, 70 are among the known failing members of neuron_reset_ahp steps D and E) have zero Chebyshev radius (pinned, slack 0); the other 6 of the 10 lowest have radius 0.44 nS and range 0.92-1.0 of the box in the hit-and-run, i.e. fat. So the polytope is thin for 4 cells per graph (40%), not for a majority.
- Delivered: confirms hubs and the cap decide capacity; shows random-draw saturation at 4-16; identifies zero-volume cells that coincide with known failing members. No new design rule; mostly reproduces lp_weights with margins added.
- Scoring: P-C1 (a) 45%: MISS (0/10; sign opposite); (b): not assessable (random vs optimised selection not comparable; reported above); cap-3T P_c <= 3 in 9/10 (80%): HIT (10/10); thin for >= 50% of the 10 worst cells (60%): MISS (4/10).

### C2 exact packing (HiGHS MILP; max disjoint 4 cue + 2 non-cue memories) vs greedy
- Formulation: binary y_C per candidate cue set (4 cells with >= 2 strong common targets outside the cue) and binary w_(C,n) per target, sum_n w = 2 y, each cell used at most once; 1,182-284,463 cue sets and 2,375-597,694 target variables per graph (s55555 smallest, s22222 largest).
- Solved to proven optimality in seconds: s55555 (1,182 cue sets): exact 2 = greedy 2 (LP relaxation 3); s66666 (2,527): **exact 3 > ovlib greedy 2** (LP relaxation 5). The feas2 packer of the repo (300 restarts) gives 2 and 3, i.e. equals the exact optimum on both solved graphs; my quick ovlib greedy undercounted by one on s66666 (and baseline feas2 3, 2, 2, 3, 4 vs ovlib greedy 2, 2, 2, 2, 3 on the other four graphs of the 5-seed set).
- **Not solved**: s33333, s11111, s77777 (17-22k cue sets) ran 42 min under a machine load of 250-570 with 20 + 10 min limits that HiGHS did not honour (killed), and the 5 graphs with 39k-284k cue sets were not attempted after the first attempt (30 greedy restarts, no limit on the LP) had to be killed. So the 10-graph decision rule (exact = greedy on >= 8 of 10) is NOT DECIDED; on the 2 solved graphs the repo's packer is exact. This formulation does not scale to the larger graphs in the time box; an exact method would need dominance reductions or a dedicated clique/biclique code.
- Delivered: a proof that the repo's packer was exact on s55555 and s66666 (K42 = 2, 3); a warning that the quick greedy variants undercount by one; no capacity increase found.

### C3 spectral design (A17 member matrices; threshold cascade; transcribed stored outcomes)
- Effective completion threshold in rho units: for any weights, completing from 13 of 17 cues needs in-sum >= (16/13) T_eff for every member, so rho >= 1.23 T_eff (rho >= min column sum). Stored data (75% cues complete at rho 15.8-16.9) give T_eff = 12.8-13.7 nS, i.e. 0.78-0.83 of T = 16.5. A random local search for the cascade-minimal matrix (400 random 13-of-17 cues, 98% complete, T_eff 13.6) finds rho 16.3 (s66666) and 16.6 (s77777), i.e. exactly the window that worked in simulation (16.0-17.1).
- **The same search for 9-of-17 (50%) cues needs rho 20.4 (s66666) and 21.3 (s77777) at T_eff 13.6 (22.5 at T_eff 15)**, against ignition at rho 16.5-17.4 (model dependent) in the stored runs. The 9-subset bound alone gives rho >= 1.78 T_eff = 23.8 for simultaneous completion of all members; the cascade (bootstrap) version is lower but still 20.4. So **a window for 50% cues does not exist for weights alone in this model class, by 20-25% of rho**. This quantifies the DER argument of MATH_REFORMULATION section 2.3; the 75% window (15.8-17.4) is about 10% wide.
- rho separates stored termination: pooled AUC 0.92 over 22 (model, design) outcomes transcribed from neuron_reset_ahp steps B2-D (script prints 0.08 because of its sign convention; the AUC of "low rho terminates" is 1 - 0.08), per-model AUC 1.0. But the threshold differs by model: terminating up to rho 16.5 (s66666, first non-terminating 16.7) and up to 17.1 (s77777, first non-terminating at 17.9-21.5). So rho is necessary but not model-independent: the per-model ceiling differs by about 4%.
- rho / d~ (d~ = sum w^2 / sum w) is 9.1-9.8 for the dense 17-cell matrices (prediction 0.5-1.5: the Chung-Lu-Vu estimate does not apply to dense small matrices).
- Not done: the log-weight convex program (D3 deviation: replaced by the cascade search and the analytic bound; the posynomial route was not worth 45 min given the answer above).
- Delivered: **strongest result of phase 1**: closes the "50% by weights alone" branch quantitatively and gives the T_eff calibration; supplies the CASC and E20 designs.
- Scoring: min rho for completion < 17.4 on >= 7/10 graphs (65%): only A17 on 2 graphs tested; 16.3 and 16.6 < 17.4: HIT for the 2 graphs tested (not 10, D2). AUC >= 0.9 (70%): HIT (0.92 pooled). rho/d~ in 0.5-1.5 (50%): MISS.

### C4 cascade / percolation
- On all 10 graphs with the as-grown unprogrammed weights (0.2 G or 0.1 G; mean in-sum 4.2-12.2 nS against T = 15) a threshold cascade never spreads: no critical seed set up to 40 of 96 cells (Monte Carlo, 60 draws per size), and the directed-configuration-model mean-field gives the same (no ignition). So spurious spread cannot come from the unprogrammed graph at all; it only exists through programmed pairs, where the model is simulation on the programmed graph (which lp_weights did, 94-96% agreement with the sims).
- Delivered: closes a branch (the formula is untestable here because there is nothing to predict: no percolation at as-grown weights). Low value.
- Scoring: formula useful on <= 3 graphs (65% that it is NOT useful): HIT (0 testable); critical random seed count >= 10 on the as-grown graph (60%): HIT (> 40).

### C5 surrogate / proxies for growth parameters
- 27 graphs (10 baselines + 17 arms of fresh_run); K42 from a quick greedy (4-5 restarts of ovlib.pack: undercounts relative to feas2, baseline 2-3 vs 2-4, so the K42 numbers are lower bounds with granularity 1). Spearman with K42: strong-pair count 0.89, top-10 strong in-degree sum 0.90, strong-graph rho 0.87, in-degree CV -0.79; within the 10 baselines only 0.52 / 0.44 / -0.40 (K42 range is 2-3). Leave-one-out linear prediction from the strong-pair count: MAE 0.47 memories (mean K42 3.6).
- Degree-preserving (in/out) surrogates of the strong-pair digraph (8 per graph, 10 swap sweeps) reproduce the greedy K42 within +-1 in 9 of 10 graphs (27182 off by 1.4).
- Meaning: cheap proxies predict arm-level capacity (they separate D 0.035 from D 0.0175 arms, which differ by 2.5x); they do not resolve within-arm differences, and the configuration surrogate matching K42 at a +-1 tolerance is a weak test with K42 in 2-3. Degree sequence plus strong-pair count carries most of what K42 sees.
- Scoring: Spearman >= 0.8 across arms (40%): HIT (0.89, n = 27); configuration surrogate within +-1 on >= 7/10 (35%): HIT (9/10, weak test).

### C6 convex relaxation: worst-case slack ranking and max-min slack
- Rank members by the worst-case 13-subset input (sum of the 13 smallest in-weights, the input a member gets from the worst 75% cue). On the E16.5 designs: s66666 ranks 57 (0), 28 (1), 77 (2), 46 (3) as the four lowest; s77777 ranks 3 (0), 65 (1), 46 (2), 67, 4 (3-4); cell 70 ranks 6. The six core failing members of steps D and E (s66666 28, 46, 57; s77777 65, 70, 3): five of six are in the lowest five, **including hub cell 3, which the failure-based list of step D missed and the mean - 1 SD rule of step E caught**. The step-E rule (below mean - 1 SD of total in-sum) selects 28, 57, 65 and 3, 65, 70: also good. The new ranking adds 77, 46 (s66666) and 46, 4 (s77777), both of which appeared among the cells missing in failed 75% events (46, 77, 79; 4, 46).
- Delivered: a new a-priori ranking at least as good as the stored rule (4 of the 5 members missing in step-E failed events are within its lowest five). Used as design W13.
- Scoring: predicts >= 4 of the 5 failing members (40%): HIT (5 of 6 core). L1-sparse variant: not run (D4).

### C7 polytope collapse under 50% cues
- Per-cell LP (cutting planes), A17 members, T_eff 13.6 and 15.0, silence level T_eff - 1, pair bounds [0.1 G, min(G, 6.86)]: for 75% cues (13 of 17) with 25% (4 of 17) silent, 13-15 of 17 members are feasible at T_eff 13.6 (11-12 at 15.0; worst slack -3.8 / -5.2 nS): **2-6 members cannot complete from a worst-case 75% cue within the grown pair ceilings**, regardless of the cap. For 50% cues (9 of 17) with 25% silent only 3-5 members are feasible at 13.6 and 2-3 at 15.0 (median slack -6.7 nS); with 37% silent 1-4. The K42 4+2 case (2 cue cells at 6.86 nS = 13.7 nS) is infeasible at T = 16.5 and 15.0 and feasible only if T_eff <= 13.7.
- Delivered: **the 50% target is infeasible in the threshold model per member (not only spectrally), and 2-6 members of A17 are 'starved' for even the 75% worst case**: this is the reason boosting the weakest members works. Hit-and-run volume ratio 75% vs 50% not computed (D5: infeasible polytopes are empty).
- Scoring: K42 50% infeasible on all graphs (95%): HIT (arithmetic); A17 9-of-17 feasible without cap on both assemblies (55%): MISS; with cap (25%): MISS.

### C8 matroid / set-packing bound (LP relaxation, swaps)
- LP relaxation of the same packing (HiGHS): s55555 3.0 vs exact 2 (bound = greedy + 1); s66666 5.0 vs exact 3 (bound = greedy + 2 against the feas2 greedy 3). The relaxation is weak: the (greedy + 1) rule holds on 1 of 2 solved graphs; 8 of 10 not assessable. Swap local search not run (D6).
- Scoring: holds on 6 of 10 (45%): not assessable (1/2).

### C9 BO / SBI / Wasserstein
- K42 seed noise: feas2 baselines 3, 2, 2, 3, 4 (SD 0.84) and pruned B1P 7, 7, 6, 7, 8 (SD 0.71) from fresh_run; my quick greedy SDs (0.42-0.45) are smaller because of coarser greedy values. Effect of D 0.035 versus 0.0175: about +4 memories. A +2 effect needs 3 growths per arm (n = 2 (1.96 + 0.84)^2 SD^2 / 2^2), +1 needs 10 per arm. **Noise is therefore not the obstacle; budget is**: a 6-parameter GP/BO needs about 90-100 evaluations (Eriksson-Poloczek rule of thumb) = 12.5 h at 3 concurrent 25-min growths. With fable-knobs' 9 instances x 3 growths (27 concurrent) it would take about 2 h of wall time, so it is feasible only on the multi-instance setup, and only for a cheap objective (K42 from feas2: minutes).
- 1-D Wasserstein distance of the strong in-degree distribution to the best graph orders the 27 graphs with Spearman 0.88 against K42 (dominated by the arm difference: baselines 4.4-5.7 vs B1P 0-2.2); not informative within an arm.
- Decision rule (noise <= half the effect and <= 60 growths): noise passes, growths (about 90) fails: NOT feasible under the rule as written; feasible with 9 instances.
- Scoring: "not feasible" (85%): PARTIAL (budget fails, noise does not; with 27 concurrent growths it is feasible).

### C10 (my addition): reduced-model design search
- rn.py reproduces the stored sim result as a baseline: E16.5 gives 75% completion 0.83 (s66666) / 0.5 (s77777) and E16.5 + boost 1.0 / 1.0 on held-out cues; 50% 0, all events end within 100 ms (6 s per 24-event batch, 7 s per generation).
- The (1+10) search over the member matrix (150 generations, soft input cap) did not find any design with 50% completion that ends: training 50% 0.0 (s66666) and 0.17 (s77777); on a held-out set of 48 events the final designs gave 75% 0.75 / 0.67 (worse than the start 1.0 / 1.0) and 50% 0/0: overfit to 24 training events, no transfer.
- Delivered: a free validator for designs (94% categorical agreement with the simulator in earlier work, and it ranks the stored designs correctly here) and a negative: no 50% window found by search in the reduced model, consistent with C3. The search result (RN) is used in phase 2 as a test of transfer.
- Scoring: 75% + termination on both (70%): HIT on the training set, MISS on held-out; 50% >= 3/6 with termination (20%): MISS (0/6, 1/6); non-uniform weights used: not assessable.

### C11 (exploratory, NOT preregistered; labelled as such, D10): E20-type designs x neuron levers in the reduced model
84 combinations (2 models x E20 scaled above the floor by 0.85/0.92/1.0 x 7 neuron arms N0, R, S2, RS2, RS3, S3, X4(sAHP 4/8 nS, outside the literature range) x r 0.56/1.0; 8 cues per class, rn.py). **No combination has 75% >= 7/8 completion with all completing events ending within 100 ms AND 50% >= 4/8 completing with ending.** The closest: s77777 f0.85 RS3 r1.0 (75% 8/8 but 0/8 end, 50% 4/8 with 6/8 ending) and s66666 f0.92 R r0.56 (50% 5/8, all 5 end, but 75% events 0/8 end). Designs where 75% events end (RS3 r0.56, f0.85) complete 0-1/8 of the 50% cues. Same trade-off as in the simulator arms of neuron_reset_ahp: 50% completion appears only with ignition (here: with 75% events not ending). Not simulated.

## Ranking by delivery (1 = most)
1. **C3 spectral**: quantified why 50% cues cannot be had from weights (rho 20.4-21.3 vs ignition 16.5-17.4), calibrated T_eff 13.4-13.7 nS, designs CASC and E20.
2. **C7 per-cell polytope under 50% cues**: per-member infeasibility, 2-6 starved members at 75% worst case, 3-5 feasible at 50% (supports C3 from a different angle).
3. **C6 slack ranking**: new a-priori boost rule (W13); predicts 5 of 6 known failing members, including the hub missed by the failure list.
4. **C2 exact packing**: proved the repo packer exact on 2 graphs (K42 2, 3); the exact formulation does not scale to the other 8 in the time box; no capacity gain.
5. **C10 reduced-model search**: free validator, negative search result, transfer test (RN).
6. **C1 per-cell LP/volume**: reproduces lp_weights with margins; zero-volume cells coincide with failing members; binding cells are the high-in-degree cells (sign opposite to the prediction).
7. **C5 surrogate**: proxies predict arm-level K42 (0.89); within-arm no.
8. **C9 BO budget**: noise is not the blocker, budget is (about 90 growths).
9. **C8 packing bound**: LP relaxation weak (5 vs exact 3 on s66666).
10. **C4 percolation**: nothing to predict (no spread at as-grown weights); formula untestable.
